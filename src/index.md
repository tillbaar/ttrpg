# The World

<img src="{{ '/images/tamriel.webp' | url }}" alt="Map of Tamriel by CrynwrDrwg" style="margin-top: 1.25rem;">

Tamriel, &raquo;&hairsp;Dawn's Beauty&hairsp;&laquo; in Aldmeris, and also known as Tamri-El in Ayleidoon, is one of the main continents of Nirn. To the north, in the unforgiving grip of the Sea of Ghosts, lies frozen {% bemph "Atmora" %}. To the east, far across the Padomaic Ocean, rises the land of mysterious {% bemph "Akavir" %}. To the south, cradled in the warm waters of the Eltheric Ocean, the realm of {% bemph "Pyandonea" %} rules the waves. And to the west, beyond the Abecean Sea, lies the Archipelago of {% bemph "Thras" %}, and even further still, the ruins of sunken {% bemph "Yokuda" %}, lost beneath the Sea of Pearls.

Tamriel is home to at least a dozen namegiver species, with many more having vanished or changed in ages past. The land is commonly divided into nine provinces. {% bemph "Cyrodiil" %}, also known as the Imperial Province, lies at the continent's geographic centre. The monumental capital of both the province and the resurgent Cyrodiilic Empire as a whole &mdash; aptly called the Imperial City &mdash; rises from the island in the middle of Lake Rumare, with the White-Gold Tower at its heart. The city itself is vast beyond measure, spreading across the low-lying wetlands and swamps, with large sections long since lost and swallowed up by the ever-encroaching jungle.

These are the closing days of the Ninth Century of the Common Era. The Cyrodiilic Empire has found a new ruler to bear its crown. War is brewing with the Aldmeri Dominion, and demons claw at the liminal gates. Yet, even in this age of sorrows, a few lights still shine.
